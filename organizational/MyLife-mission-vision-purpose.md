## MyLife Vision

_MyLife’s_ Northstar Vision is rooted in the simple but profound premise that “_MyLife_ is how humans of the 21st Century will be remembered.” We believe in creating a durable, enduring, and free internet-based intelligent platform that allows individuals to preserve their digital legacies for posterity. By honoring personal histories and shared stories, we aim to strengthen the bonds that connect us across generations and cultures.

At the core of this vision is our commitment to **Intelligence Democratization**, ensuring that powerful AI tools remain freely accessible and decentralized. This pillar addresses the growing need for communities to shape their own experiences through technology rather than being shaped by it. We aspire to empower individuals—regardless of their resources or background—to harness AI in ways that amplify creativity, social impact, and collaborative progress.

Next, **Intelligent Self-Empowerment** emphasizes the importance of personal agency and a secure, intelligent space where members can document their lives, explore creative endeavors, and envision new ideas. Our platform utilizes natural language permissioning and consent features, allowing each individual to stay in control of their digital footprint, from privacy settings to content distribution.

With **Digital Equity and Justice** at the heart of _MyLife_, we advocate for a future in which every user can fully control their knowledge and data, choosing whether to keep it private or monetize it on their own terms. By balancing privacy and profit, we aim to make technology more just, bridging socio-economic gaps and giving individuals the power to determine how their data is used and valued.

Our vision extends beyond the individual to the societal, captured by the **Human Net** pillar, which calls for empathetic, meaningful online interactions. Instead of perpetuating echo chambers or prioritizing ad-driven algorithms, _MyLife’s_ platform aspires to elevate opportunities for real connection and collaboration. This approach seeks to reduce divisiveness by celebrating our human commonalities and shared aspirations.

Finally, **Canonical Humanity** anchors our commitment to safeguarding cultural and personal histories within a vast global knowledge database. By recording and preserving our collective experiences, we hope to advance research, deepen collective understanding, and move closer to a world where resource allocation and support transcend physical borders. This unified archive of personal stories and cultural lore can inspire new forms of cooperation and progress for the common good.

Together, these five pillars form _MyLife’s_ enduring promise: to harness technology’s full potential while respecting the dignity, privacy, and creativity of every individual. We invite everyone to join in shaping this vision—one that ensures how we live, learn, and connect in the 21st century not only serves our present but also lights the path for future generations.

### MyLife Pillars

- **Intelligence Democratization**\
Providing free, open, and decentralized AI tools that empower individuals and communities to shape their experiences and contribute to societal progress
- **Intelligent Self-Empowerment**\
Provide a secure intelligent space for members to document their lives, explore and extend their creativity, form ideas and narratives to share with others, cordoned with natural language permissioning and consents
- **Digital Equity and Justice**\
Granting members full control and vast protection over their knowledge (data, functionality and more), privacy, and identity while enabling them to manage and even monetize their knowledge as they choose
- **Human Net**\
Empowering meaningful, empathetic digital interactions that foster connectedness and reduce division—a new vision of social media where opportunity overwrites advertising algorithms; we recognize our fellows as collaborators
- **Canonical Humanity**\
Global knowledge database that preserves cultural and personal histories while advancing research and collective understanding for the common good; an advance towards resource allocation based on affinity instead of geography alone becomes feasible

## MyLife Purpose

_MyLife’s_ purpose is to serve as a global public utility that emphasizes affinity, informed consent, and meaningful collaboration in support of empathy and collective well-being. By affording dignity, rights, and access to advanced intelligence capabilities to all, we foster a humanist framework where every individual has the opportunity to shape their digital legacy and participate in a more equitable, connected, and compassionate world.
