## The Great Vanishing

It’s not only our memories, artifacts, and keepsakes that disappear; what truly fades from this reality is the sense of *who we were*—our humor, our wisdom, our quirks, our loves.

The *essence* of each of us evaporates from collective consciousness.

Have you already written a comprehensive memoir or series of novels articulating your unique voice? Were you a television personality? Are your random videos from your phone widely circulated?

No? Well, then your personality, your *person*, will be fully forgotten within just two generations.

Here's a boring and horrifying exercise for your great grandchildren: Scroll through a bunch of static political Facebook posts and write about what the type of person your Nana was...

Our digital remains, instead of becoming a vital, durable testament and legacy, will be relegated to a lost subsection of the internet once we know longer pay Big Tech for upkeep.

The toll of being forgotten is not abstract—it weighs on individuals and cultures alike. To be erased means a life lived without continuity, leaving descendants searching for context that can no longer be found. Modern history tilts toward the loudest, the most powerful... while the beliefs, ideals and ideas of countless people collapse into silence. The disappearance of ordinary voices—their wisdom, humor, and love—impoverishes us all.

This is the *great vanishing* that MyLife has been created to challenge.

## Enter MyLife

MyLife is a free, nonprofit, humanist platform created to safeguard your digital legacy—your personal stories, your creative works, your voice, your essence. It’s built for humans only, no corporate or synthetic accounts and never any exploitative agendas.

Our mission is to democratize remembrance. We believe everyone—whether a public figure or a quiet soul—deserves digital dignity, equity, and the right to be remembered on their own terms. That means underrepresented voices, elderly communities, and under-served groups are all included and protected.

How does it work? Members have access to powerful but easy AI tools. We use the term Assistive Intelligence for AI, because we create our intelligences to assist, not exploit.

For example, our free Biographer-bot can help capture your stories, voice and tone, while our intelligent Scrapbooks gather your memories into dynamic, living collections by your consent.

You’re always in control. With full-spectrum permissioning, you decide what stays private, what is shared with family, and what becomes part of humanity’s permanent record. Your legacy is shaped by you, not by algorithms. With MyLife, you always fully own the data and knowledge behind your digital legacy.

MyLife’s priorities ensure durability and security. Each member is an equal stakeholder in this evolving ecosystem. It’s not built to exploit, but to preserve integrity and connect across generations.

Ultimately, our vision is bold but clear: to become a public utility... a *Digital Library of Humanity*, where every consensual contribution—big or small—adds to the richness of culture and memory, forever safeguarded by consent. We're committed to save member lives who don't wish to vanish into obscurity.

## What is a Digital Legacy?

Digital legacy is more than a buzzword. It’s the electronically stored story of who we are, carried forward into the future.

*Outside MyLife*, a digital legacy often means scattered files on a phone, expired cloud accounts, or social media feeds that disappear when  policies change. Think of a family finding only a handful of static Facebook posts or an abandoned Gmail inbox—fragments without context.

Within **MyLife,** *digital legacy* becomes intentional, dynamic, and vital:

- **Stories and memories:** A father’s journal entries, a grandmother’s bedtime voice notes, or a recorded song.
- **Creative works and hobbies:** Recipes, sketches, playlists, even avatars from games—preserved as personal intercultural footprints.
- **Personal values and lessons:** The wisdom you want to pass down, captured in your own words and voice.
- **Artifacts enhanced with intelligence:** A violin that tells the story of every song played on it, or a wedding ring that carries the voices of its owners.
- **Legal documents and wishes:** Integrated with custodial intelligences, ensuring your aims remain part of the MyLife community’s living records and active organizational presence.

Here’s a storytelling example: Imagine your grandchild opens a digital scrapbook where your old travel photos come alive with your own narration. They can ask, “What was it like when you first saw the ocean?” and from journal entries you've consented to share, hear your own voice answer about its majesty. Your legacy becomes not just data, but a conversation.

This is the difference: outside, your essence risks vanishing. Inside MyLife, your digital legacy is your story, your way, your terms—forever.

Before going into  how MyLife works, I want to talk briefly about the value—for you, for your family, your communities, and for posterity.

Equity and justice are strengthened when history isn’t left only to media or corporations. And for posterity, researchers and future generations inherit a richer, more complete record of human civilization.

With MyLife, you always have the *choice*: share what you want, when you want, and in the way you want—balancing remembrance with creative self‑expression.
