### MyLife Grant Dimensions

We identify 4 dimensions along which we understand how to classify and organize our grant materials as well as grant _outcomes_.

- Research
- Technical
- Education
- Outreach

This section focuses on projects that haven't been constructed yet and are in ideation phase; it can also be a post-mortem on current functionality. In either case, we would expect as an outcome some yield to go into technical specifications for further evolution of the platform.

- Feasibility Studies & Exploratory Research
- Technical Standards & Frameworks
- Impact Studies
- Knowledge Dissemination: conference seminars, white papers, etc.

General advice: Let the market decide, we have wide lenses - look for affinity and collaboration as there are a great number of vantages through which to look for synchrony.

#### Technical Definition

In the `Technical` category, I am focused on the following type of engineering projects that have technical specifications prepared, be they loose in GitHub or far more precise.

- Platform Development
- Infrastructure
- Systems Upgrades
- Pilot Implementations & Releases

#### Education Definition

Here I'm thinking of the following types of outcomes:

- Published white papers, manuals, or toolkits
- Online/multimedia courses
- Workshops, seminars, or conference presentations
- Assessment reports tracking educational impact

The fields of focus therefore might be: Informational Content Creation, Professional Development, and Public Awareness

#### Outreach Definition

Board Development (pre-bootcamp) is not dependent on these, but opens access to the possibility of increasing size and diversity of person and mind.
Here will we focus on opportunities around:

- Community Building & Membership
- Awareness & Advocacy
- Partnership & Network Building
- Marketing & Communications

The type of deliverables I expect in this category are things like:

- Public-facing events or workshops with measurable attendance/impact
- Partnerships with nonprofits, local organizations, or industry leaders
- Volunteer programs or advisory boards for sustained engagement
- Comprehensive outreach strategies or communication plans

#### Human-Generated Content Marketplace/UBI (Research)

- Technical Standards & Frameworks for User-Centric Micro-Payments
  - **Concept**: Develop and test open standards that allow seamless, low-cost micro-payments to creators for their contributions (text, images, art, etc.).
  - **Key Activities**
    - Research existing frameworks (blockchain-based micropayments, fiat microtransactions, NFT licensing).
    - Engage stakeholders (developers, artists, policymakers) to design interoperable protocols and fair licensing models.
    - Draft technical reference implementations or APIs.
  - **Potential Outcomes**
    - Open-source tools or libraries documenting best practices for implementing secure, frictionless micropayment systems.
    - Industry workshops or consortium-driven standard-setting events to encourage widespread adoption.
- Feasibility Study on a Human-Generated Content UBI Model
  - **Concept**: Investigate whether a marketplace for user-generated content can provide a viable path to Universal Basic Income. Examine economic models, tokenomics, and platform dynamics.
  - **Key Activities**
    - Assess micro-transaction systems, royalty splits, and revenue-sharing approaches.
    - Conduct pilot studies or simulations with real user participation.
    - Evaluate legal, regulatory, and technological barriers.
  - **Potential Outcomes**
    - Evidence-based white paper or toolkit summarizing viability, challenges, and recommended next steps.
    - Foundation for large-scale pilot or pilot expansions in collaboration with partners (e.g., technology providers, local communities).
- Impact Study on Equitable Content Compensation
  - **Concept**: Measure how equitably a human-generated content marketplace distributes earnings, especially among marginalized creators. Explore how it impacts socio-economic disparities or fosters new economic opportunities.
  - **Key Activities**
    - Collect data from creators (income levels, demographics, content types) to analyze distribution patterns.
    - Examine social and community impacts, including whether the system encourages collaboration or deepens existing inequalities.
    - Interview key stakeholders, including platform operators, creators, and end-users.
  - **Potential Outcomes**
    - Robust research report or policy brief identifying success factors and pitfalls in marketplace design.
    - Recommendations for policy or platform adjustments to enhance inclusion, equity, and user empowerment.
- Pilot Collaboration with Existing Creator Platforms
  - **Concept**: Partner with established social media or content-creation platforms to test research-driven UBI models (e.g., partial revenue sharing, token-based rewards).
  - **Key Activities**
    - Integrate a pilot revenue-sharing mechanism into a small subset of platform users.
    - Collect real-time data on user engagement, satisfaction, and perceived fairness.
    - Analyze whether the shift in compensation structure changes behavior, retention, or content quality.
  - **Potential Outcomes**
    - Detailed pilot study results showing potential adoption barriers, user acceptance rates, and monetization effectiveness.
    - Framework for scaling the approach across larger user bases or additional platforms.

#### Digital Legacy Educational Programs

- White Paper: “Understanding Digital Legacy in the 21st Century”
  - Format: A professionally formatted document for broad distribution
  - Focus: Defining Digital Legacy – Clarify key terms, concepts, and real-world examples
    - Legal and Ethical Dimensions
    – Provide an overview of personal data rights, estate planning considerations, and future implications.
  - Why MyLife?
    – Highlight MyLife’s human-centric intelligent platform and how it addresses emerging digital legacy challenges.
  - Outcome: A reference document that educates stakeholders (users, academics, policymakers) on the importance of preserving one’s digital presence and how MyLife offers unique advantages.
- User Guide & Toolkit: “Managing Your Digital Legacy with MyLife”
  - Format: Printable or downloadable manual plus interactive checklists
  - Focus:
    - Step-by-Step Management – Walk users through MyLife’s tools, from account setup to selecting privacy settings and deciding on legacy permissions.
    - Value Proposition – Outline MyLife’s core features (consent frameworks, AI-driven archiving, user empowerment).
    - FAQ & Best Practices – Address common questions (security concerns, data usage, ownership) and offer tips for long-term planning.
  - Outcome: Practical guidance that empowers individuals to start building and maintaining a robust digital legacy, highlighting MyLife’s difference.
- Online Courses & Multimedia eLearning Series
  - Format: Self-paced modules or short video tutorials
  - Focus: What Is Digital Legacy? – Introduce the concept with compelling stories, statistics, and case studies.
    - MyLife Basics – Tutorials on navigating the platform, using AI tools, and customizing user preferences.
    - Ethical & Cultural Perspectives – Invite expert speakers to discuss the global implications of digital legacy, especially how various cultures handle memories and inheritance.
  - Outcome: Accessible, interactive learning that accommodates different learning styles, fosters digital literacy, and frames MyLife as a trustworthy, user-focused service.
- Workshops, Webinars, or Conference Presentations
  - Format: Live or virtual sessions in collaboration with libraries, universities, or tech conferences
  - Focus:
    - Digital Legacy 101 – Introduce participants to concepts like data inheritance, privacy, and the ethical responsibilities of storing personal memories online.
    - Managing with MyLife – Demonstrate platform features and answer real-time questions on functionality, trust, and potential use cases.
  - Why MyLife? – Make the case for a human-centric approach: a platform built on consent, empathy, and collaborative knowledge.
  - Outcome: Immediate engagement with target audiences; builds credibility, community awareness, and user confidence in the MyLife platform.
- Assessment & Impact Reporting
  - Format: Evaluation framework or outcome-based studies
  - Focus:
    - Measuring Educational Impact – Track user comprehension before and after workshops or courses (e.g., use surveys or quizzes).
    - Platform Adoption Metrics – Assess how many participants start using MyLife or share it with family and friends.
    - Long-Term Behavior Changes – Analyze if participants develop ongoing habits of digital content archiving, privacy optimization, and estate planning.
  - Outcome: Data-driven insights that demonstrate the value of educational initiatives, help refine content, and guide resource allocation for future projects.
