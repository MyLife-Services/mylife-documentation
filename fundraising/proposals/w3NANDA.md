# NANDA W3 Proposal

## MyLife Web3 Transition

MyLife proposes a real-world transition from a nonprofit human-centric ethical AI Web2 platform to a Web3 node, establishing a consent-driven, decentralized registry for human memory agent-intelligences *as well as* NANDA. This project will demonstrate practical pathways for user-controlled data storage, agent discoverability and vitality, and cross-registry interoperability, while also hosting and maintaining registry instances that contribute directly to the NANDA Registry Quilt ecosystem. With MyLife ensuring consent-based privacy in a federated Web3 environment, this meta-collective of first-hand human stories can help researchers collaborate to explore how individuals overcome challenges, shape communities, experience cultural shifts, and much more.

## Project Context

### Problem Statement

Today’s digital platforms predominantly operate in centralized silos, where individuals have limited control over their personal data and where registries of autonomous AI agents are fragmented and isolated. MyLife is a live, nonprofit platform that already helps members curate and share their life stories but currently operates on a centralized Web2 architecture. This leaves the platform’s digital legacies vulnerable to the same trust, portability, and security limitations that NANDA W3 seeks to solve.

MyLife’s transition to Web3 is not only necessary for its own mission—it provides a real-world use case to showcase how human-centered agent registries can ethically participate in a federated, decentralized quilt of registries.

From its inception, MyLife was built to champion human data and knowledge ownership. Our organizational design anticipates the need for individual control over both personal data and agentic intelligences. True data verity, agency, and cross-platform viability can only be achieved through Web3 primitives that decentralize storage, governance, and agent registries. This transition is not simply technical—it is foundational to fulfilling our mission of preserving human stories with integrity, portability, and privacy.

### Solution Overview

MyLife proposes a practical Web3 transition that includes establishing and hosting decentralized registry instances for human memory agents within the NANDA Registry Quilt. We will empower members with self-owned, decentralized storage (Solid Pods or IPFS) and develop a consent-driven access and governance system using smart contracts and human-readable consent tools. By participating fully in the Quilt, MyLife will help enable seamless agent discoverability, registry interoperability, and human-centered data flows across platforms.

### MyLife’s Commitment to the NANDA Ecosystem

MyLife has been on NANDA's registry since its start. We are currently found as two MCP servers (one for visitors, one for members) and are finalizing incorporation of A2A.

We believe that the agentic Quilt is more than a technical solution—it is a cultural model for human connectivity in the 21st century. The Quilt represents the next evolution of human networks where individuals, their agents, and their stories can travel, connect, and contribute across federated systems without sacrificing privacy or agency. MyLife intends to participate in this ecosystem with great vigor—as a trusted node, a collaborative partner, and an active builder of open, interoperable pathways for all.

## Proposed Solution and Approach

MyLife proposes to transition from a centralized Web2 platform to an interoperable Web3 node by addressing the following, and more to be discovered:

* Establishing and hosting decentralized registry instances for human memory agents within the NANDA Registry Quilt.
* Implementing Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs) and Verifiable Credentials (VCs) to ensure secure agent authentication and cross-registry discovery.
* Empowering members with self-owned, decentralized storage using Solid Pods or IPFS.
* Developing a consent-driven access system using smart contracts and natural language permissions.
* **Stretch-Goal**: Participate in Quilt governance through a human-centered DAO to ensure ethical registry stewardship.
  * Why a stretch?\
  *We consider this not to be an after-thought or a checkbox, it's a meaningful obligation we intend to commit to when ready. Our position is that DAO participation should follow a successful Web3 infrastructure migration and the stabilization of registry hosting. Once those foundations are in place, we can responsibly embark on this armature.*

MyLife will offer both a transition model for current users and an open, extensible framework for future Quilt participants.

### Criteria for Success

We will measure technical success along the following dimensions:

* **Interoperability**: MyLife will adopt DIDComm and W3C-compliant protocols to enable seamless cross-registry communication, agent discoverability, and shared authentication.
* **Privacy**: Consent-based access will be enforced through smart contracts, with privacy maintained by decentralized, member-controlled storage and user-owned keys.
* **Scalability**: Leveraging decentralized storage and modular APIs, MyLife’s node will support growth across both agents and registries without bottlenecks.
* **Trust**: Smart contract logs, transparent governance, and verifiable credentials will establish auditable, cross-platform trust across the Quilt ecosystem.

### Implementation Considerations and Open Questions

* UI/UX: How can we make decentralized identity and storage systems easily accessible to non-technical users? How much can be instantiated on-demand?
* Governance Design: How can a human-centered DAO maintain inclusivity and transparency while scaling to support global participants? This consideration is in research phase.
* Storage Selection: Which decentralized storage option(s) will best balance long-term archival needs with accessibility, especially for multi-generational storytelling?

## Current Development Status

MyLife’s Alpha MVP has been proofed and validated, and the platform is actively awaiting Beta deployment. The current platform is Azure-hosted, utilizing Cosmos DB and an established DevOps pipeline. Major next-phase development arms include:

* Web3 transition and registry node integration
* Mobile skin and accessibility layer
* Enhanced member experience for narrative presentation
* Communal Memories feature for collaborative, multi-member storytelling

MyLife is positioned for rapid progression into Web3 interoperability and Quilt participation.
