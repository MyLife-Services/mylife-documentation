# Cosmos Grant

Today’s digital memories are often fragmented, subjective, and easily distorted by platform algorithms or bad actors—obscuring collective truth and eroding trust. *Truth in Memory* harnesses ethical, consent-driven AI on the *MyLife* platform to enable users and communities to collaboratively refine, verify, and preserve personal and shared narratives, creating a transparent, authentic archive for future generations.

## Truth in Memory through Collaborative Narratives Grant

### Project Alignment with Cosmos × FIRE Principles

*Truth in Memory* embraces the core principle of fostering open inquiry through AI tools that amplify rigorous, collaborative examination of truth. Aligning with Cosmos × FIRE’s commitment to challenging intellectual complacency, the project utilizes ethical AI to transparently question and refine personal and collective narratives.

*MyLife's* approach leverages assistive intelligence (AI) to help individuals collect, shape, and share memories as part of their digital legacies, controlled by user-defined consent. Shared memories become collaboratively refined narratives through AI-mediated interactions, allowing visitors to thoughtfully contribute their perspectives. Each collaborative memory is assigned its *own relational intelligence*, preserving context, intent, and consent, while managing the incorporation of diverse viewpoints. From this, a unique truth emerges from multi-perspective, collaborative, consensual, and mediated dialogue.

### Statement of the Problem

Today's digital memories are fragmented, subjective, and increasingly vulnerable to distortion by small and large entities. Existing social platforms often reinforce confirmation biases and isolate communities, thereby obscuring collective truths. Additionally, there is a lack of effective, transparent tools that allow individuals to collaboratively examine, refine, and verify personal narratives against broader collective experiences and established facts.

### Project Plan

"Truth in Memory" will be a functional prototype developed as extended functionality of the existing *MyLife* AI platform. The project will comprise custom code development and deployment, technical documentation, and demonstration tools. The platform prototype will be fully operational, allowing individual members to collaboratively build and refine memories, and optionally participate in anonymized or identifiable evaluations to gain deeper insights.

### Success Metrics

We evaluate success through multiple dimensions, tracking key indicators across these primary categories:

* **Engagement Metrics**
  * Total number of memories created (by members vs. visitors)
  * Average number of collaborators per memory
  * Frequency of revisits and edits per memory
  * Conversion rate from visitor to active collaborator/member
* **Usage Metrics**
  * Total and average interactions (comments, edits, contributions) per session
  * Rate of new memory creation per active user
  * Ratio of private to public memories created
* **Quality & Depth Metrics**
  * Average depth (e.g., word-count, density of details) per memory
  * Percentage of memories enhanced after AI prompts
  * Average length of collaborative chains (iterations of edits and responses)
* **Veracity Metrics**
  * Truthfulness index (alignment of user-generated content with external verified data)
  * Average deviation in truthfulness between original and collaborative versions
  * Frequency and success rate of AI-driven verification prompts
* **Relational & Collaboration Metrics**
  * Number and effectiveness of relational intelligence agents deployed
  * AI-measured empathy and understanding within collaborative memory discussions
  * Consistency and coherence scores for relational memory representations
* **System Performance Metrics**
  * System uptime and availability percentage
  * Average latency of AI-generated prompts and responses
  * User satisfaction scores related to AI responsiveness and accuracy

"Truth in Memory" will demonstrate an effective model for fostering collective truth through collaborative, consent-driven AI interactions.
