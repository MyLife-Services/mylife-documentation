# Grant Programs

## MyLife Mission

MyLife is how humans of the 21st Century will be remembered.
MyLife is committed to providing humanity a durable, enduring and free internet-based intelligent platform to preserve an individual’s digital legacy for posterity.
Be remembered.

- Intelligence Democratization
- Intelligent Self-Empowerment
- Digital Dignity, Equity and Justice
- Intelligent Human-Centric Network
